Badminton Training

Badminton Skills Academy

Visual guides detailing wrist snaps, lunging geometries, and court positioning to speed up shuttlecock deflections.

01

Wrist Flick & Forearm Rotation

Master the pronation swing. Rapidly rotate your forearm inwards right before hitting the shuttlecock to generate maximum wrist snap speeds. Keep your shoulder socket relaxed.

Forearm Pronation Snap: Rapidly pronate the forearm inwards 90 degrees right at impact to redirect deflections.
Loose Grip Control: Hold racquet handle loosely until 0.1s before contact to increase recoil snap.
Target Velocity: Speeds up high racquet snap actions for smash speeds up to 120 mph.
02

Split Step Agility Footwork

Perform a micro-hop right as your opponent strikes the shuttlecock. This pre-activates your calves and allows you to lunge instantly towards all four court boundaries.

Split Pre-Hop Timing: Time the micro-hop bounce exactly as the opponent racquet meets shuttlecock.
Low Stance Agility: Maintain feet wider than shoulders and low center of gravity to activate calves.
Explosive Directional Launch: Push off the outer foot instantly to drive towards the court boundaries.
