SS Sports

Sareen Sports (SS) was established in 1979. SS has hand-pressed willow bats for generations of world champions, delivering power and balance.

SG Cricket

Sanspareils Greenlands (SG) was founded in 1931. SG represents the standard for Test cricket balls and high-durability leg guards.

MRF Sports

Madras Rubber Factory (MRF) entered cricket in 1989. Sponsoring elite international batting icons with shock-guard willow designs.

Adidas

Founded in 1949. Adidas brings primeknit ventilation and carbon-engineered sole structures to athletics.

Nike

Established in 1964. Sourcing lightweight flyknit weaves and pressurized air-sole cells for track shoes.

Puma

Founded in 1948. Puma integrates lightweight nitro foams and carbon flex plates to ensure explosive sprint starts.

Yonex

Established in 1946. Yonex pioneered isometric court rackets and lightweight carbon-graphite flex shafts.
