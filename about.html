A Passion for Precision

AeroVolt was created in 2018 by a group of materials engineers and professional cricket coaches who realized that off-the-shelf equipment rarely matched the strict demands of professional varsity players.

What started as a single workbench tuning handles and compression grains quickly evolved. Today, we collaborate directly with top global factories to source raw willow, graphite composites, and knit outsoles before tuning them in our localized calibration experience centers.
