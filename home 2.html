Ballistic Dynamics

COG BALANCING PHYSICS RULES

Understand how shifting the center of gravity (COG) changes swing weight parameters, torque generation, and kinetic energy transfers.

Head-Light Balance

Shifts weight close to the handle grip. Maximizes wrist maneuverability, acceleration index, and reaction speeds. Best suited for defensive playstyles.

Even Balance

Distributes weight equally along the chassis. Offers a symmetrical sweet spot, balancing offensive speed and control indexes.

Head-Heavy Balance

Shifts weight to the hitting zone. Increases rotational torque and exit momentum, amplifying dynamic impact velocities.
